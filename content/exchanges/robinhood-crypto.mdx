Robinhood Crypto is the digital asset arm of Robinhood Markets, the US brokerage that made commission-free stock trading mainstream, and it sits inside the same app as equities and options. It is the most beginner-accessible route into crypto for an American retail user, and it has spent 2025 and 2026 turning itself from a simple buy-and-sell screen into something considerably more ambitious: a public blockchain, tokenized equities in 120 countries, and an events-contract business that now out-earns its crypto trading desk.

## The Brokerage Approach, and What It Costs You

Robinhood came to crypto from equities, and that shapes everything about the product.

There is no separate account, no exchange interface and no learning curve: **[Bitcoin](/posts/what-is-bitcoin)** and **[Ethereum](/posts/what-is-ethereum)** sit next to your stocks with the same order flow and the same mobile-first design. For someone whose alternative was abandoning a crypto exchange sign-up halfway through, that friction removal is the whole value proposition.

The cost is embedded rather than displayed. Robinhood does not charge a headline commission on crypto trades; it earns from the spread and from order routing. That is legitimate and it is also less legible than a published maker-taker schedule, which means a user cannot easily compare what they paid against **[Coinbase](/exchanges/coinbase)** or **[Kraken](/exchanges/kraken)**. The absence of a visible fee is not the absence of a fee.

The catalogue is also deliberately narrow relative to global venues, reflecting a US-regulated brokerage's caution about which **[altcoins](/posts/what-are-altcoins)** it will list.

## Buying Bitstamp, and Becoming Global

Robinhood's most consequential crypto decision was acquiring Bitstamp, completed in June 2025.

Bitstamp is one of the oldest exchanges in the industry, with a genuine institutional business and licences across Europe, the UK and Asia. Robinhood had a large US retail base and essentially no institutional or international crypto presence. The acquisition bought both at once, and Robinhood has reported that Bitstamp institutional volumes more than doubled after the deal closed.

That transaction is why the availability line on this page changed. Robinhood Crypto remains a US retail product, and the group behind it now operates a licensed exchange across multiple jurisdictions serving professional counterparties. Those are different businesses sharing an owner, and which one you are actually a customer of depends on where you live.

## Robinhood Chain and Tokenized Stocks

In July 2026 Robinhood launched the public mainnet of Robinhood Chain, a layer-2 network built on Arbitrum aimed at tokenized real-world assets and **[DeFi](/posts/what-is-defi)** applications. Since launch it has processed more than USD 12 billion in decentralised exchange volume across over 150 million transactions.

The chain exists to support Stock Tokens, tokenized equity exposure now live through Robinhood Wallet in more than 120 countries, with the European offering quadrupled to roughly 2,000 instruments. A publicly listed US brokerage issuing tokenized shares of other public companies on its own **[blockchain](/posts/what-is-blockchain)** is a genuinely novel arrangement, and it is not available to US customers.

Two cautions belong here. A Stock Token is a derivative claim referencing a share, not the share itself, so shareholder rights, dividend treatment and what happens in an insolvency all differ from direct ownership in ways the marketing does not foreground. And a brokerage-operated layer 2 is a centralised chain in every practical sense, whatever its settlement layer, which makes it a distribution channel rather than a decentralisation story.

## Prediction Markets Have Overtaken Crypto Trading

The most revealing number in Robinhood's recent disclosures has nothing to do with tokens.

Event-contract revenue, the prediction markets business, reached USD 156 million in the second quarter of 2026, overtaking crypto trading's USD 100 million as crypto volumes softened across the industry. Analysts covering the stock now treat tokenization and prediction markets, alongside Bitstamp and Robinhood Earn, as the company's principal growth drivers rather than spot crypto.

For a user of this platform that matters strategically. Robinhood is not a crypto company that also sells other products; it is a retail trading company for which crypto is one line among several, and one whose relative contribution is currently shrinking. Product attention, and eventually product investment, follows revenue.

## Regulation, Custody and the Staking Question

Robinhood Crypto operates as a FinCEN-registered money services business, holds a New York BitLicense and maintains state money transmitter licences, which puts it under meaningful US oversight compared with offshore venues. It has also had regulatory friction: the SEC issued a Wells notice concerning its crypto business in 2024, and the company has been vocal about the absence of clear rules.

Custody is the part users most often misunderstand. Assets in the main app are held custodially by Robinhood, not by you, and they carry no FDIC or SIPC protection. SIPC covers securities in a brokerage failure; it does not cover crypto. Holding Bitcoin in the same app as your SIPC-protected stocks does not extend that protection to the Bitcoin.

The separate Robinhood Wallet application does offer genuine self-custody, where you hold the keys. That is the right destination for anything you are not actively trading, and treating the brokerage as a venue rather than a vault is the single most useful habit here. **[Staking](/posts/crypto-staking)** availability has varied with US regulatory conditions, and **[proof of stake](/posts/what-is-proof-of-stake)** rewards on a custodial platform mean your assets are being deployed by someone else on your behalf.

## What to Weigh Before Using It

- **Work out what the spread actually costs you.** Compare the all-in price of a trade against a published fee schedule elsewhere. Commission-free is a pricing structure, not a discount.
- **Do not confuse SIPC with crypto protection.** Your stocks are covered in a brokerage failure. Your Bitcoin is not, and sitting in the same app changes nothing.
- **Move long-term holdings to the wallet.** The self-custody app exists and is the correct place for them. If you are unsure **[how to store crypto](/posts/how-to-store-crypto)**, resolve that before the position gets large.
- **Stock Tokens are not stock.** If you use them outside the US, read what you are actually buying and how it behaves if the issuer fails.
- **Expect the product to follow the revenue.** With prediction markets now out-earning crypto trading, crypto is not where this company's strategic energy is concentrated.
- **The app makes trading easy, which is the risk.** Frictionless interfaces and gamified design encourage activity, and activity is how retail loses. The decision between **[trading and holding](/posts/crypto-trading-vs-holding)** is worth making deliberately rather than by default, and platform convenience does not protect you from **[crypto scams](/posts/how-to-avoid-crypto-scams)** aimed at you directly.

## Who Robinhood Crypto Suits

It suits US beginners who already hold stocks in the app and want modest crypto exposure without opening an account somewhere new, and who value simplicity over cost and breadth. For that person it is a reasonable and legitimately regulated starting point, and **[crypto for beginners](/posts/crypto-for-beginners)** paired with this app is a lower-risk entry than most alternatives.

It suits active traders poorly. The spread-based pricing is opaque, the asset list is narrow, and there is no order book, no advanced order types and no API worth the name. Anyone trading with frequency will do better on a venue that publishes its fees, and **[understanding crypto exchanges](/posts/understanding-crypto-exchanges)** as businesses is the step that makes that comparison possible.

The more interesting question is what Robinhood becomes. Between Bitstamp's licences, a live layer 2, tokenized equities in over 120 countries and an events business now larger than its crypto desk, this is no longer a brokerage that added a Bitcoin button. It is a retail trading conglomerate assembling the pieces to tokenize its own core product, and the crypto app most of its users see is the smallest part of that.
