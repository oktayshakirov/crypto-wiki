## edgeX: An Order-Book Perp DEX for Crypto, Stocks and Commodities

edgeX is a non-custodial **[decentralized exchange](/posts/what-is-a-dex)** for **[perpetual futures](/posts/what-are-perpetual-futures)** on crypto, US and Korean stocks and commodities, incubated by the Hong Kong trading firm Amber Group. It was founded in March 2024, opened to the public in November 2024 on a layer 2 settled to Ethereum, and since mid-2026 has run on its own application-specific chain, EDGE Chain. Its native token, EDGE, launched on 31 March 2026.

For a stretch of late 2025 edgeX was one of the biggest venues in on-chain derivatives. A research report that The Block published in partnership with edgeX put its November 2025 volume at a record 167.2 billion US dollars, the fourth-largest of any perp DEX that month. A year later the picture is very different. On 11 November 2026 it ranked ninth by open interest among the 25 perp DEXs tracked by Castle Labs, with about 154.7 million dollars, and its monthly fees had fallen by around 95 percent from their peak.

That arc, a fast rise built on incentives and a mobile-first product, followed by a disputed airdrop and a token crash, is the most useful thing to understand before you trade on edgeX.

## From StarkEx Validium to EDGE Chain

edgeX came out of Amber Group, a digital asset firm founded in 2017 and headquartered in Hong Kong. Amber incubated the project and led its pre-seed round in December 2024, for an undisclosed amount. Co-founder Kevin Wang has said the founding team spent more than six years building derivatives products for institutional clients, and team members have previously worked at Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, Barclays and **[Bybit](/exchanges/bybit)**. The company had about 40 employees in February 2026.

The first version of the exchange launched on testnet in August 2024 and to the public in November 2024. It ran a central limit order book on a validium built with StarkWare's StarkEx engine: orders were matched off-chain, and the results were settled on **[Ethereum](/posts/what-is-ethereum)** with zero-knowledge proofs. Growth came fast in 2025. According to DefiLlama, edgeX earned about 1.4 million dollars in fees in April 2025 and about 63.8 million dollars in November 2025, its best month so far.

In January 2026 Circle Ventures, the venture arm of the USDC issuer Circle, invested an undisclosed sum as the sole investor in a new round, announced on 10 February 2026. Circle said it planned to bring native USDC and its cross-chain transfer protocol to EDGE Chain. At the time edgeX reported more than 295,000 users, about 5 billion dollars in daily volume and roughly 1 billion dollars in open interest.

The V2 upgrade went live around the start of June 2026. It moved trading onto EDGE Chain, an application-specific chain built with Arbitrum infrastructure, and added round-the-clock markets on US equities, Korean equities and commodities, isolated margin and TWAP orders. edgeX says the V2 engine handles up to 300,000 orders per second with order latency below 100 milliseconds. Those are the company's own figures, not independently measured ones.

## Core Protocol: How edgeX Works

edgeX works like a centralized exchange on the surface and settles like a DEX underneath. You connect a self-custody wallet, deposit collateral and trade against a central limit order book, while the exchange's contracts hold the funds.

- **Crypto Perpetuals:** The core market, covering Bitcoin, Ether, Solana, BNB and a long tail of smaller tokens, with leverage of up to 100x on the major pairs. Funding is settled hourly. High leverage means a small move against you can trigger a **[crypto liquidation](/posts/what-is-a-crypto-liquidation)**, and that risk does not shrink because the venue is on-chain.
- **Stock and Commodity Perpetuals:** V2 lists more than 40 pairs across US stocks, Korean stocks and commodities, trading 24 hours a day, seven days a week. These are derivatives that track a price, not **[tokenized stocks](/posts/what-are-tokenized-stocks)** that represent a share, so you get no voting rights or dividends. Gold and silver were among the most actively traded of these markets in early 2026.
- **Spot Trading:** A smaller spot market sits alongside the perpetuals.
- **Prediction Markets:** edgeX has partnered with Polymarket to offer **[prediction markets](/posts/what-are-prediction-markets)** inside the app.
- **Vaults and Sub-Accounts:** Users can put capital into strategy vaults, some of which have a lock-up window, and run up to 20 sub-accounts under one unified-margin account.
- **Fees:** In January 2026 the base rate was 0.012 percent for makers and 0.038 percent for takers, falling with 30-day volume. edgeX pays the gas for settling trades, and since V2 its Trade to Own program has paid part of trading fees back to users in EDGE.

On depth, edgeX has made strong claims. The same sponsored report cited Artemis data from 7 November 2025 showing about 126 BTC of bitcoin liquidity within one basis point of the mid price, against about 44 BTC on **[Hyperliquid](/exchanges/hyperliquid)**. Order-book depth changes quickly, and with open interest down sharply since then, treat that comparison as a snapshot from edgeX's peak rather than a current one.

## The EDGE Token, the Airdrop Dispute and the June Crash

EDGE launched on 31 March 2026 with a fixed supply of 1 billion. The published allocation was 35 percent for the ecosystem and community, 25 percent for core contributors, 25 percent for a genesis **[airdrop](/posts/understanding-crypto-airdrops)** to past users, 10 percent for the foundation and up to 5 percent for a pre-launch season. The team and foundation shares were locked at launch.

The airdrop became a controversy within weeks. In April 2026 on-chain analysts flagged more than 80 new wallets that they alleged were linked to the team, which together received around 180 million EDGE, worth more than 90 million dollars at the time. The Chinese outlet Odaily argued that regular traders received only about 4 percent of supply, against the 25 percent the community had been promised, and crypto.news and Odaily reported that edgeX restricted comments and muted members who questioned the distribution. These are analyst findings and allegations, not confirmed results, and edgeX has not published an account that resolves them. After the backlash edgeX reportedly locked about 141.6 million EDGE, roughly 14 percent of supply.

The token peaked at about 1.51 dollars on 21 May 2026. On 2 June 2026 it fell from roughly 1.20 dollars to an intraday low of about 0.37 dollars, a drop of around 70 percent. edgeX said it had not been hacked and blamed "deliberate attempts by certain external party to manipulate the market price of EDGE." The on-chain investigator ZachXBT disputed that, arguing that a small group of insiders controlled most of the supply and that the low float made such a move possible. About 350 million EDGE were in circulation at the time. Messari listed EDGE at about 0.33 dollars on 29 October 2026.

edgeX says it puts 100 percent of its net profit toward buying EDGE on the open market. DefiLlama records more than 40 million dollars of buybacks in the second quarter of 2026 and about 2 million dollars in the third, a drop that follows the fall in fees. A buyback is only as large as the revenue behind it, and that revenue has shrunk.

## Security Measures

edgeX is non-custodial. Its terms of use state that only your own keys can control your assets, that edgeX does not hold those keys, and that lost keys cannot be recovered. That removes the risk of an exchange operator misusing customer funds, but it puts the whole job of key security on you, starting with your **[seed phrase](/posts/what-is-a-seed-phrase)**.

The platform has published audits from SlowMist and Rigsec, and a PeckShield audit of the StarkEx v4.5 system that V1 ran on, in a public GitHub repository. Under StarkEx, users had a forced-withdrawal path to take funds out on-chain if the front end stopped responding. EDGE Chain is a newer, separate system built on Arbitrum infrastructure, and we found no independent review of its exit mechanics as of November 2026, so do not assume the V1 guarantees carry over unchanged.

As of November 2026 we found no publicly reported hack of the exchange itself. The incidents in edgeX's record so far concern its token and governance: the airdrop allegations and the June 2026 crash covered above. Some analysts have also questioned how much of edgeX's 2025 volume came from organic trading rather than incentive farming, a question that applies across many points-driven perp DEXs.

## User Experience

edgeX built mobile into the product early. About 40 percent of its trading volume ran through its native iOS and Android apps, according to the January 2026 report, and the apps cover trading, positions, vaults, deposits and withdrawals. On iOS the publisher is EDGEX DAO PTE. LTD., and on Google Play the app is listed as "edgeX" by edgeX Protocol. Search for those names and check the developer before installing, because an unrelated App Store app called EdgeX Network comes from a different company.

There is no account signup and no identity verification. You connect a wallet, deposit collateral and trade, which is quick for experienced users but leaves no support desk that can reverse a mistaken transfer. The interface is aimed at active derivatives traders, with sub-accounts, isolated and cross margin, TWAP orders and an API for programmatic trading. It is not a beginner's on-ramp.

Among the on-chain perp venues already on this site, edgeX sits between **[Lighter](/exchanges/lighter)**, which leads with zero retail fees and verifiable matching, and **[Aster](/exchanges/aster)**, which offers the widest range of markets. Hyperliquid remains far larger than all of them, with about 55 percent of tracked perp DEX open interest on 11 November 2026.

## Decentralization and the Regulatory Landscape

edgeX does not hold a financial licence, and it does not present itself as a regulated exchange. Its terms of use name edgeX Trading Inc. as the contracting party, apply California law and send disputes to arbitration in San Francisco.

Access is limited by those terms rather than by a licensing regime. People and companies located or incorporated in the United States or Canada are blocked from the interface, and residents of sanctioned territories, including Iran, Cuba, North Korea, Syria, Myanmar and the Crimea, Donetsk and Luhansk regions, are barred from the services. Using a VPN to get around those limits breaks the terms.

The stock and commodity markets add a second layer of risk. Leveraged derivatives on listed equities are tightly regulated products in most countries, and an unlicensed venue offering them to retail traders operates in a grey zone that regulators could tighten at any time.

## Points to Consider

- **A Shrinking Venue:** Fees fell from about 63.8 million dollars in November 2025 to about 2.3 million dollars in October 2026 on DefiLlama's figures, and total value locked from about 249 million to about 59 million dollars. Lower activity usually means thinner books and wider spreads, especially in smaller markets.
- **Token Governance Questions:** The airdrop allegations and the June 2026 crash are unresolved. If you hold EDGE, treat it as a high-risk asset with concentrated supply, and **[do your own research](/posts/do-your-own-research)** before buying.
- **Leverage Cuts Both Ways:** Up to 100x on majors means very small price moves can close a position. Size trades for the worst case, not the best.
- **New Chain, New Assumptions:** EDGE Chain is young. Its exit guarantees and decentralization are less tested than the StarkEx setup edgeX started on.
- **Not for US or Canadian Users:** Both countries are blocked outright, and the terms forbid VPN workarounds.

## Conclusion

edgeX is a capable order-book perp DEX with a real product behind it: a strong mobile app, round-the-clock markets on crypto, stocks and commodities, and Amber Group's trading experience in its DNA. In late 2025 it was briefly one of the four largest venues in on-chain derivatives, and the V2 move to EDGE Chain gave it more room to build.

Its 2026 record is the harder part. A disputed airdrop, a token that lost most of its value in a day, and a steep fall in fees and deposits have pushed it out of the top tier. For experienced derivatives traders outside the US and Canada who want stock and commodity perpetuals in a self-custody app, edgeX is worth a look. For anyone weighing the EDGE token, or looking for the deepest and most proven on-chain venue, the risks and the alternatives deserve more weight than the 2025 headlines.
